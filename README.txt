Thornhold: Relics of the Warlord
================================

To play on this computer: open index.html in a web browser
(Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari). A phone may be cramped;
a computer works best.

This is the same pass-and-play game. One device, take turns.
It is not online multiplayer — each person who opens the link
or the file has their own copy.

Hover a card, or tap the magnifying-glass button, to see the
full painted card.
